# Evidence Synthesis

## Pillar 1
SOURCE FACT: Government policy material frames logistics efficiency as integrated, seamless, reliable and cost-effective. Historical PIB material cited private estimates around 13–14% of GDP and explicitly noted there was no official estimate at that time. A later PIB update said official estimation work was being undertaken and cited a range of 8–14% from prior studies. Therefore the UI must not present "13–14% is the official current number" as fact.

SOURCE FACT: MoPSW's 2024-25 Annual Report states Sagarmala's vision is to reduce logistics costs for domestic and EXIM cargo with minimal infrastructure investment. Sagarmala guidelines include coastal shipping and inland waterways among programme objectives.

ENGINEERING INFERENCE: The strongest policy-aligned feature is not a decorative "Sagarmala" badge. It is a comparable scenario engine that can show domestic/coastal/import alternatives, assumptions, and cost consequences.

SOURCE FACT: Ministry of Coal currently publishes Coal Directory 2024-25 chapters and the full directory.
ENGINEERING INFERENCE: Coal statistics should be used as historical/reference evidence, while current commercial freight/commodity values remain timestamped inputs or verified feeds.

## Pillar 2
SOURCE FACT: IMD exposes official API documentation for warning-related and cyclone-related data.
ENGINEERING INFERENCE: Hazard layers should be adapter-based and must show source timestamp and last successful refresh.
SOURCE FACT: MapLibre GL JS is an open-source browser map library; PMTiles support demonstrates a path toward packageable/offline data.
ENGINEERING INFERENCE: Static ports, reference corridors and chokepoints can remain available without network access. Live official layers degrade gracefully.

## Pillar 3
SOURCE FACT: Department of Expenditure publishes a current GFR 2017 compilation updated through 31 January 2026.
ENGINEERING INFERENCE: The app should not claim "GFR compliant" merely because it contains a banner. It should provide traceability, authority-controlled workflow, frozen evidence and reproducible justification.

## Pillar 4
SOURCE FACT: Paradip publishes berth-specific limits and high-tide conditions. For example, its New Coal Import Berth is listed with 300 m LOA, 46 m beam and 16 m draft, with 16 m berthing during high tide; Coal Berth-01 is listed at 14.5 m base draft with up to 16 m during high tide.
ENGINEERING INFERENCE: Port eligibility must be berth-aware and conditional. A single "port max draft" field is not sufficient.
SOURCE FACT: Visakhapatnam publishes berth information and periodical draft restrictions.
ENGINEERING INFERENCE: constraints require effective-date/version metadata.

## Pillar 5
ENGINEERING INFERENCE: An executive command center should surface decision blockers, uncertainty, provenance and pending approval before decorative charts. This directly connects the five pillars into a coherent decision flow.
