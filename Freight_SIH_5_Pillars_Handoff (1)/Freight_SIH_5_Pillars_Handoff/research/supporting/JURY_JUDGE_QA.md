Q: Are you claiming government approval?
A: No. The platform aligns features to published policy objectives and preserves source evidence; it does not claim endorsement.

Q: Is all map data live?
A: No. Each layer is classified as static reference, official periodic, model output, user input or demo simulation.

Q: Is delay equal to demurrage?
A: No. Delay exposure is a financial estimate. Contractual demurrage requires contractual laytime/rate inputs.

Q: Can the AI approve a charter?
A: No. It is decision support. Approval is a human workflow action.

Q: Why berth-level constraints?
A: Official port publications demonstrate that LOA/beam/draft and high-tide conditions can vary by berth.

Q: Why cost per GJ?
A: It normalizes procurement economics by usable energy instead of comparing only nominal cost per tonne.
